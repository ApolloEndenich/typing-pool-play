A deduction voyage · sketch

Solve the case, read the hands, jump.

The map to the Hidden Star was torn into pieces. At every starport one traveller carries a piece. Work out who is who from the customs photos or the cargo ledger, send your answer to the relay, then read the next coordinates on the crew's fingers and jump. The last piece leads to the treasure.

Choose how to play: Photos (customs camera spots, the easiest to start with) or Ledger, written in Words, Pictures or Scenes, with plain numbers or alien hand numerals. Play a whole Voyage, or just Single puzzles with no map and no jumps. Voyages come in four lengths: Quick and Short (3 aliens, 3 piers), Standard (4 and 4) and Long (5 and 5). Today's case is the same for everyone today. Helps lets you switch colour coding, hints and the X-ray on or off, and choose how hard the finger counting is, from Toddler to Full. More under How to play, at the bottom.

        How to play
        
Who is who, at every pier

      

      
    
        
  • The cards are the aliens on the pier, Alien A, B, C… Tap one to see its hands up close. For each alien, pick a name, a home planet and what it carries.
    • 
        
  • Photos: the customs camera caught each alien at one spot. A spot shows only part of an alien, such as the antennae, the eyes, a hand or the markings. A speech bubble gives a name, and a see-through suitcase shows the cargo and a home-planet sticker. Each photo is one moment, so an alien shows up at most once in it: two spots in the same photo are always two different aliens. Tap a spot to look closer. ✕ puts a spot or a whole photo aside when you're done with it, and ↺ brings it back.
    • 
        
  • Ledger: the same kind of case as a list of entries, in Words, Pictures or Scenes. When an entry compares two aliens (more, fewer or the same), they are always two different aliens. ✓ ticks an entry off.
    • 
        
  • Stars say how hard a case is. ★ Easy: every step needs one or two entries (or photo spots). ★★ Medium: one step needs three. ★★★ Hard: a step needs four or more, or the case needs a "suppose" (try a value and watch it clash). ★★★★ Very hard: three or more "suppose" steps. Long ledger cases are mostly ★★★.
    • 
        
  • The relay confirms correctly identified aliens two at a time and never says which ones are wrong. Hint points at the next real deduction. Press it again (Explain) to see it worked out step by step, and once more (Show me) to fill that answer in. The counter at the top keeps all three: hints, explained, shown. The X-ray shows what one alien carries, once per case.
    • 
        
  • The coordinates: the alien with the map piece has its crew hold up fingers, and each crew member is one digit in that species' counting. Pick the matching star and jump. Counting, under Helps, has four levels: Toddler (one crew member, count the raised fingers), Kids (two members, add all their raised fingers), Youth (two members, two places in their base) and Full (up to three places).
