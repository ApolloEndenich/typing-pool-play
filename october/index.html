    On a keyboard: 1 to 4 choose a leaf, 0 the eraser, U or Ctrl+Z undo, H hint.
    
    
    Stars say how hard a board is, from its own hints. ★ Easy: every step follows from touching leaves or one number. ★★ Medium: up to three steps need several numbers at once. ★★★ Hard: up to eight. ★★★★ Very hard: nine or more.
    
    
    The daily board's size goes with the weekday: 21 leaves on Monday and Tuesday, 34 on Wednesday and Thursday, 55 on Friday and Saturday, and 89 on Sunday.
    
    
    Every board is made by a generator and proved by a solver to have exactly one answer, which you can reach without guessing. The leaves are drawn in code.
    
    
    Made for the UofT GDDC Fall 2026 Game Jam, theme COLOR. Generative AI, as the jam asks: built with Claude Code (Anthropic), which wrote the code, the generator and solver, and the words, directed and playtested by us. Every leaf is drawn by that code: no image generator, no asset packs.
