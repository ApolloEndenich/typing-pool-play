Trick or Croak

Halloween on Gourd Lane.
One sweet, one croak, six pumpkins.

How Gourd Lane works

  1. Every child tells the truth about what was at their eye level. Tall children see pumpkins. Small children see hems. Every child knows which treat they got.
  2. Pumpkins and bags come off only at Widow Krall's apple tub. You can't bob for apples with a pumpkin on, so each zombie sets its pumpkin on its own fence post, carved face turned toward Widow Krall's house, and its bag beside it. Widow Krall watches from upstairs: she sees pumpkins, never hems.
  3. A zombie can't see its own pumpkin. From the lane, every pumpkin on a post shows the same round orange back, so each zombie takes whatever is on its own post, and once it's on, it can't see the face. Nobody looks in a bag either. Only a naughty zombie sneaks round the posts to peek at the faces.
  5. Two zombies hand out each kind of treat from their bags. A hem always names its zombie: robes never come off.

The whole lane comes out, and the night begins. Tap any zombie on the street to look closer.
